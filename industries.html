Industries We Serve

Delivering specialty lubricants and technical expertise to diverse manufacturing sectors across Maharashtra. Our solutions help reduce downtime, extend equipment life, and improve operational efficiency.

Industry Sectors

Automotive

High-performance lubricants for engine assembly lines, transmission systems, and precision machining operations. Our specialty greases and metalworking fluids ensure smooth production of automotive components while meeting stringent OEM specifications for bearing lubrication, gear oils, and assembly pastes.

  • Engine & transmission assembly greases
  • Precision bearing lubricants
  • Metalworking & cutting fluids

Steel

Extreme-temperature lubricants designed for continuous casting, hot rolling mills, and heavy-duty steel processing equipment. Our products withstand temperatures exceeding 1000°C while providing reliable protection for bearings, chains, and gears operating in harsh, high-load environments typical of integrated steel plants.

  • High-temperature chain & conveyor oils
  • Rolling mill bearing greases
  • Continuous casting mould lubricants

Cement

Robust lubrication solutions for crushers, kilns, ball mills, and vertical roller mills operating under extreme dust, vibration, and thermal stress. Our specialty products resist contamination and washout, ensuring reliable performance of open gears, kiln support rollers, and heavy-duty bearings in abrasive cement manufacturing environments.

  • Open gear & kiln lubricants
  • Ball mill & crusher bearing greases
  • Dust-resistant conveyor chain oils

Food Processing

NSF H1 registered food-grade lubricants that comply with FDA 21 CFR 178.3570 regulations for incidental food contact. Our range covers chain oils, bearing greases, and hydraulic fluids suitable for bakeries, dairy plants, beverage bottling lines, and edible oil processing facilities where hygiene and safety are paramount.

  • Food-grade chain & conveyor lubricants
  • NSF H1 bearing greases
  • Hygienic hydraulic & compressor oils

Textile

Stain-free and low-residue lubricants specifically formulated for spinning frames, looms, knitting machines, and dyeing equipment. Our textile-grade products prevent fabric contamination while providing excellent wear protection for high-speed spindles, needle bearings, and chain drives operating at elevated temperatures.

  • Spindle & loom bearing oils
  • Stain-free knitting machine greases
  • High-speed needle bearing lubricants

Pharmaceutical

Cleanroom-compatible and FDA-compliant lubricants for tablet presses, capsule filling machines, blister packaging lines, and HVAC systems in controlled environments. Our pharmaceutical-grade products meet strict purity standards, are odourless and tasteless, and support GMP compliance in drug manufacturing facilities.

  • Tablet press & capsule machine lubricants
  • Cleanroom-compatible greases
  • GMP-compliant packaging line oils

Power Generation

Reliable lubrication solutions for turbines, generators, transformers, and auxiliary equipment in thermal, hydro, and wind power plants. Our products deliver long service life under continuous operation, protecting critical assets like turbine bearings, generator seals, and switchgear mechanisms from wear, corrosion, and oxidation.

  • Turbine & generator bearing oils
  • Transformer & switchgear lubricants
  • Wind turbine gear & bearing greases
