About Essential Lubricants

Your trusted partner for industrial lubrication solutions in Maharashtra since establishment. Authorized channel partner of Kluber Lubrication India Pvt Ltd and BASF Chemetall India Pvt Ltd.

Company Overview

Essential Lubricants is a leading industrial lubrication solutions provider operating across the Maharashtra region, with a strong presence in Aurangabad, Jalna, Khamgaon, Malkapur, Burhanpur, Latur, and Nanded. We specialize in supplying high-performance specialty lubricants, metalworking fluids, and surface treatment chemicals to manufacturing industries.

As an authorized channel partner of globally renowned brands, we bring world-class lubrication technology to local industries. Our deep understanding of regional manufacturing needs, combined with access to premium products, enables us to deliver tailored solutions that improve equipment reliability, reduce maintenance costs, and enhance operational efficiency.

We serve a diverse range of industries including automotive, steel, cement, food processing, textile, pharmaceutical, and power generation sectors. Our team of experienced professionals provides comprehensive technical support, from initial lubrication surveys to ongoing oil analysis and product recommendations.

Our Partner Credentials

We are proud to be authorized channel partners of industry-leading lubrication and surface treatment brands.

Kluber Lubrication India Pvt Ltd

Authorized Channel Partner

Essential Lubricants is an authorized channel partner of Kluber Lubrication India Pvt Ltd, a Freudenberg Group company and one of the world's leading manufacturers of specialty lubricants. We supply their complete range of high-performance greases, oils, and pastes for demanding industrial applications across the Maharashtra region.

  • Specialty greases for high-temperature and high-speed applications
  • Synthetic lubricating oils for gears and bearings
  • Assembly and running-in pastes
  • Food-grade lubricants (NSF H1 certified)

BASF Chemetall India Pvt Ltd

Authorized Channel Partner

Essential Lubricants is an authorized channel partner of BASF Chemetall India Pvt Ltd, a global leader in surface treatment technologies and metalworking fluids. We provide their comprehensive range of cutting fluids, rust preventives, and cleaners to manufacturing plants throughout the Marathwada region.

  • Metalworking fluids and cutting oils
  • Rust preventive solutions
  • Industrial cleaners and degreasers
  • Surface treatment chemicals

Lawas Lube Specialties

Authorized Distributor

We also distribute products from Lawas Lube Specialties, offering cost-effective lubrication solutions for general industrial applications. Their product range complements our premium offerings, ensuring we can meet the needs of every budget and application requirement.
