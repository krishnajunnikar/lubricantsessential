Our Services

Comprehensive lubrication solutions to maximize equipment reliability, reduce maintenance costs, and extend machinery life across your manufacturing operations.

Service Offerings

Lubrication Surveys

Our expert engineers conduct comprehensive on-site lubrication surveys to assess your current lubrication practices and identify areas for improvement. We evaluate equipment condition, lubricant selection, application methods, and storage practices to develop a tailored lubrication management plan that reduces unplanned downtime and extends equipment life.

  • Identify incorrect lubricant applications
  • Reduce unplanned equipment downtime
  • Optimize lubricant inventory and consumption
  • Extend machinery service life
Request a Survey

Oil & Grease Analysis

Our laboratory-based oil and grease analysis service provides detailed insights into lubricant condition and equipment wear patterns. By testing samples for viscosity, contamination, wear metals, and degradation, we help you predict potential failures before they occur and optimize oil change intervals to reduce waste and maintenance costs.

  • Predict equipment failures before they occur
  • Optimize oil change intervals
  • Reduce maintenance costs by up to 30%
  • Monitor contamination and wear trends
Schedule Analysis

Technical Support

Our dedicated technical support team provides expert guidance on lubrication challenges, troubleshooting, and best practices for your specific industrial applications. Whether you need help resolving a bearing failure, selecting the right grease for extreme temperatures, or training your maintenance staff, our engineers are available to assist with practical, field-proven solutions.

  • Expert troubleshooting for lubrication issues
  • On-site training for maintenance teams
  • Application-specific guidance from Kluber specialists
  • Rapid response for critical equipment problems
